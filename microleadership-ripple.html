    The line marks 100: enough to be funded.

      Grounded in Alex Budak, “Microleadership: How to be a Leader When You Are Not the Leader” (alexbudak.com), the basis of his TEDx talk “You don't have to be a CEO to be a leader”. The team, the scheme and every scene are fictional.

      Rules

      Get the pitch to 100 readiness by Friday at 4pm, and build a team that leads itself. You have no title. Kit, the coordinator, has one.

      Each day

      1. Spot it. Three everyday scenes. Call each one micro-leadership, micromanagement, or neither. Get all three right and you see more chances to act: +1 energy today.
      2. Your moves. You get 3 energy a day. Each moment offers responses with an energy cost; what they do is revealed after you choose. You cannot afford everything, so choose where to lead. Unspent energy becomes solo work: +2 readiness each.
      3. End of day. Each teammate adds readiness according to the trust they had that morning, so trust you build today pays from tomorrow. Any teammate at trust 4 or more starts micro-leading: each one lifts their least-trusted colleague by 1.

      Scoring

      • +10 for each scene you classify correctly (15 scenes).
      • +1 per readiness point gained, −1 per point lost.
      • +5 per trust point a teammate gains, −5 per point lost, ripples included.
      • Friday: +50 if readiness reaches 100, and +10 for every teammate who is micro-leading.
